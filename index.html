About FOB

We all remember that moment when Azeroth finally clicked.

The first town that somehow felt like home. The first dungeon that made your heart beat a little faster. The first time you realized just how enormous this world really was. That strange, wonderful feeling of looking over the horizon and thinking, "I wonder what's over there?"

That's the feeling we're chasing.

FOB was created around the idea that World of Warcraft is a world to experience, not a spreadsheet to conquer. We're here to quest, explore, get lost, run dungeons, tell ridiculous stories, role-play, help each other, and occasionally make decisions that seemed much better five minutes earlier.

No mandatory min-maxing. No racing to the finish line. No worrying about whether you're playing the "right" way. If you want to stop and admire a sunset, investigate a suspicious cave, spend an afternoon fishing, or spend forty minutes trying to reach somewhere you probably weren't supposed to be... you're among friends.

The idea started with wanting to show my wife the Azeroth I remember loving, and hopefully rediscovering it along the way. But maybe we'll find something else too.

New friends.
Old friends returning from another chapter.
New adventures we never could have planned.

FOB is intended to be a welcoming home for players of every experience level, from the wide-eyed adventurer stepping into Azeroth for the first time to the grizzled veteran who still remembers when their bags were permanently full of vendor trash.

And yes, we're a cross-faction family. Horde or Alliance, different characters, different worlds, different adventures... but the same fire.

We'll run dungeons. We'll enjoy endgame. We'll organize events, contests, role-playing adventures, exploration nights, and whatever other wonderfully questionable ideas the family comes up with.

Sometimes we'll accomplish something amazing. Sometimes we'll get hopelessly lost. Sometimes the entire evening will consist of standing around talking in a tavern.

All of it counts.

So pull up a chair, adventurer. There's room around the fire.
