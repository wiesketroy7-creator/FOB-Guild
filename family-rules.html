FAMILY RULES

OF ENGAGEMENT

1. REAL LIFE COMES FIRST

Family, work, health, and real-world responsibilities always come before the game. No guilt. No pressure.

2. FOB IS A CROSS-FACTION FAMILY

Horde or Alliance, different characters, different worlds, different adventures... but the same family. Respect everyone regardless of faction.

3. BE RESPECTFUL

Zero tolerance for harassment, racism, bigotry, threats, sexual harassment, or hateful behavior. Treat the people behind the characters with respect.

4. KEEP POLITICS OUT OF FOB

Politics and ideological fighting stay out of guild chat, Discord, and in-game spaces. We're here to escape the noise, not recreate it.

5. SUPPORT EACH OTHER

Help when you can. Ask for help when you need it. Be patient with new players and veterans alike. We're a family, not a competition.

6. PLAY YOUR WAY

Quest. Dungeon. Raid. Explore. Role-play. Fish. Craft. Get hopelessly lost. Whatever you enjoy, there's room around the fire.

7. KEEP IT FUN

We're here for adventure, laughter, good stories, and questionable decisions. Don't forget why we're here in the first place.

HORDE. ALLIANCE. DOESN'T MATTER. THERE'S ROOM AROUND THE FIRE.

FAMILY OVER BULLSHIT
